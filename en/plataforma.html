Digitized role

The macro-tasks and groups that make up the role, with business objectives built in. Alerts when a macro-task stops receiving entries. Rol Vivo isn't a fixed org chart: what the business needs today, current responsibilities, the aspirational vision of where the role should grow, and the gap between what's asked of the person and what they can actually sustain all live in the same place.

Tracking and deep reflection

Events come in from the calendar (Google or Outlook) and are mapped against the role, with AI-assisted classification — the person corrects and confirms, they don't transcribe by hand. Each event can open into a deep reflection: how well that hour matched the role, whether it should have been delegated, whether it generated real value.

Management dashboard and deviations

Weekly hours, daily average against target, role-match percentage, distribution by macro-task, and objectives with no consumption. It's not a vanity dashboard: every number is designed to become a decision — reorganizing a schedule, delegating a task, having a specific conversation with a team member.

Artificial intelligence with a purpose

Trained on our DNA

This isn't a generic model answering questions about your calendar. Pausa's agents are trained on the methodology, on more than seventeen years of experience in management and inner work, and on the knowledge accumulated by the platform.

Insights Agent

Generates actionable recommendations for conscious, aligned management — it cross-references each person's focus and deviation data and suggests which conversation to have or what to reorganize, before anyone has to ask.

Assisted classification

Suggests which macro-task to map each event against, based on history, context, and meeting attendees. Reduces the manual tracking workload to a one-click confirmation.

Management chat

Create, update, or query the role in natural language, without navigating the interface — for the leader who wants a quick answer without opening five screens.
