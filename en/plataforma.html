How it works

See clearly. Decide what to change. Notice the difference.

Pausa is designed around a cycle that repeats every week: awareness, action, and impact.

01 · Awareness

Where you are today, versus where you want to be.

Living role is your role made visible: the MacroTasks that make it up, grouped, and how much time you want to give each group. The Role Assistant helps you build it, and you adjust it when your work changes.

The agenda records where your week went, and the report compares it with your living role. For example, in Product:
