Goals and priorities

From business goal to this week’s calendar.

  • Every time goal carries its business goal: “Increase the conversion rate by 65%” next to “Product: at least 20% of the week.”
  • Every Monday, Weekly focus breaks that goal down into two or three concrete focuses, with suggested blocks in the calendar.
  • At the end of the week, each focus is marked Fulfilled, Not completed or Lost priority: you see whether time went to what matters.

Organization

And all of this, across the whole organization.

What each leader sees about their own week, leadership sees across the whole organization: by team, by leader and over time. No spreadsheets or hand-built reports.

  • Role fit for each team, side by side: where the gray areas are and where time is going to the wrong place.
  • Focus achieved and average logged per team, to see which leaders stick to their priorities.
  • An AI analysis of how leaders are doing in the period, with suggested next steps.
