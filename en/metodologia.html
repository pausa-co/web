C

Consciencia

“Before acting, you need to know with clarity where you stand — and where you should be headed.”

What questions does it answer?

  • What should I be doing?
  • What am I actually doing?
  • What management gaps exist between my reality and what I should be doing?

Three levels, three tools

1

What should I be doing? — Role, Foco Semanal, AI recommendations.

2

What am I actually doing? — Tracking, schedule, AI powered.

3

What management gaps are there? — Reporting, AI Agent, overconsumption and underconsumption.

A

Acción

“Consciencia without Acción is just diagnosis. Acting is what moves the needle.”

Implementation

We're with you from day one of the Método CAI

Intensity decreases as adoption matures. The goal isn't for Pausa to always be hands-on, but for the habit to stick.

Months 1–2

Onboarding

Consciencia takes hold: role, tracking, and first reports, with intensive support throughout the process.

Months 3–5

Consolidation

The organization begins to Act with autonomy: the habit is already installed and the focus shifts to leading with data.

From month 6

Sustainability

Support focused on sustaining the habit and multiplying the Impacto.

The timeline for each stage is an estimate and assumes an uninterrupted cadence.
