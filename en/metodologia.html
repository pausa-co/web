Pausa Methodology

Without a Rol Vivo there is no deviation. Without deviation there is no insight. Without insight there is no transformation.

Three steps that repeat every week, and an artificial intelligence trained to sustain them — not to replace them.

01

Build the Rol Vivo

“Not a perfect role, but a living, agile one.”

How do we measure it?

  • Role digitized in the platform.
  • Modifications to the role throughout the process.
  • Definition and modification of objectives.

What does the Rol Vivo include?

1

What the business and strategy ask of them today.

2

Their current responsibilities.

3

Their aspirational vision: what they should ideally be doing.

4

What they're being asked to do and can do.

5

What they're being asked to do and can't do.

6

Task groups and macro-tasks as an ideal reference.

02

Understand the focus

“Crossing the inputs with the Rol Vivo reveals the real gap between where the focus was and where it should have been.”

How do we measure it?

  • Amount and quality of tracked time.
  • Under- and over-consumption of objectives.
  • Tasks logged as "No," "Unsure," or "Should delegate."

What do we draw on?

Input: what each person tracks.

Sources: Google / Outlook Calendar (intermediate tracking) and the Pausa platform. Logging is assisted by the classification agent: the person corrects and reflects, not transcribes.

03

Identify deviations and action items

“See clearly the gaps between reality and the Rol Vivo, and identify concrete possibilities.”

How do we measure it?

  • Number of deep reflections.
  • Time spent on leadership tasks.
  • Number of insights sent from the app.
  • Structural decisions made.
  • Evolution of "Not my responsibility" and "Should delegate."

Types of intervention

Deep reflection

Structural decisions

Delegation and managing the NO

Calendar reorganization

Data-centric conversations with leaders

Identifying inefficient tasks

Artificial intelligence with purpose

Trained on our DNA, not on generic data

This isn't a generic model answering questions about your calendar. Pausa's agents are trained on the methodology, on more than seventeen years of experience in management and interiority, and on the accumulated knowledge of the platform. Each one sustains a different step of the mechanism:

Sustains step 02 · Foco

Assisted classification

Suggests which macro-task to map each event to, based on history, context, and meeting attendees. Reduces manual tracking to a one-click confirmation.

Sustains step 03 · Desvío

Insights Agent

Cross-references each person's focus and deviation data and generates actionable recommendations: what conversation to have, what to reorganize, before anyone has to ask.

Sustains step 01 · Rol Vivo

Management chat

Create, update, or query a role in natural language, without navigating the interface — for the leader who wants to adjust someone's Rol Vivo without opening five screens.

Implementation

We're with you from your first Rol Vivo

Intensity decreases as adoption matures. The goal isn't for Pausa to always be hands-on, but for the habit to stick.

Months 1–2

Onboarding

The Rol Vivo is built: role, tracking, and first reports, with intensive support throughout the process.

Months 3–5

Consolidation

The organization begins identifying deviations with autonomy: the habit is already installed and the focus shifts to leading with data.

From month 6

Sustainability

Support focused on sustaining the habit and multiplying action items.

The timeline for each stage is an estimate and assumes an uninterrupted cadence.
